Date: 2026-11-17. Source of truth: live MRI scan of the product (36 functions, 60 routes, graph fresh today) and `git log origin/main` through #1466, checked against every public page and `lib/for-your-ai.ts`.

## Part 2. Shipped, and the site barely shows it

This is the undersell. Everything here is live on main today.

**Receipts, the full story.** Every answer carries which memory it stood on, which rules fired, which model answered and what it cost. The site says "receipts" and never shows one. This is the single strongest proof we have. Lead with it.

**Teach a rule from any answer.** Highlight a reply, turn it into a standing rule, the system follows it from then on. Not on the site at all. This is "a company that trains its own mind" in one click.

**Experts at the table.** Bring a named expert into a chat by name and it shapes the reply. Hire one by interview. It studies your memory and forms its rules. The site mentions experts once and never shows the interview or the seat.

**The Inbox.** Everything your experts and agents found lands in one list you clear a card at a time: keep, teach, promise, not useful. The product's daily loop. Not on the site.

**Team page.** Experts and agents are one roster, each with a job description, who they work for, who is waiting on you, and what they spent this month. Not on the site.

**Workflows in one sentence.** When, who, does, asks me, unless. With a map and a run history. Not on the site.

**Agents agree or disagree.** When two agents find the same thing or clash, the system says so instead of showing both. Not on the site.

**Jobs held for your yes.** When the system wants to act in your name it holds the job and waits. The site says "a person confirms" and never shows the held job.

**Blockers and blind spots.** What is in the way of each goal and what you may have missed. Not on the site.

**Ask your data with no AI in the loop.** "Top ten calls by time in March" answered straight from the database, no model, no estimate. Not on the site. Finance buyers will love this.

**Connect any API without code.** Point it at an API with a key, say what to pull, test it, save. Plus ready connectors: HubSpot, Pipedrive, Stripe, Mailchimp, Semrush, Airtable, Notion, Jotform, Typeform. Site never names one.

**Drop a file.** A spreadsheet or an exported chat history becomes memory. Attach a PDF, deck or photo in chat and it reads it. Site lists "documents" as a source and stops.

**Privacy veto.** Tell it never to use a given person or subject and that holds everywhere. Not on the site.

**Remove a source and everything it made.** With a log. Not on the site.

**Who-saw-what, for real.** A record of every time the system read a topic, every reply's sources, every agent decision. The site says it in words. Show the record.

**It checks itself.** The system maps its own parts every day and reports when the deployed code drifts from the repo. A trust line for the CTO page.

**Correct a fact and it sticks.** Fix one wrong fact or merge two topics and the whole memory follows. Site mentions corrections once.

Suggested home for each: receipts, teach, Inbox, held jobs → home and /product. Experts at the table, Team, workflows, agree/disagree → /product Agents and Experts sections. Ask your data, cost per teammate → /for/finance. Any-API, named connectors, drop a file, veto, remove, who-saw-what, self-check → /for/technology and /security.

## Part 4. What the video may show

Every caption under ten words, no numbers, no names of real people, demo account only. Beats in the flywheel order Matt gave.

1. **Own it.** "Your company's memory. Yours, not rented." (true: plain Postgres, your keys, your license)
2. **Any model behind it.** "Pick the model for each job. Change it anytime." (true: per-job model settings, Claude, DeepSeek, GLM, your own server for reading). Do not say "run chat locally".
3. **Pulse.** "Every morning: what moved, what's waiting on you." (true: /pulse)
4. **Chat.** "Ask. It answers from everything you know." (true)
5. **Receipt.** "Click any answer. See where it came from, what it cost." (true, new this week)
6. **Teach.** "Highlight an answer. Make it a rule." (true)
7. **Memory.** "Every fact, dated and sourced, filed on its own." then zoom out: "Topics form themselves as you work." (true)
8. **Promises.** "What you promised, pulled from the conversation." (true)
9. **Experts.** "Hire an expert by interview. Seat it in any chat." (true)
10. **Agents.** "Teammates with a job description and a schedule." then "They find things. You keep, teach, or skip." (true: Team, Inbox)
11. **Confirm.** "Nothing goes out in your name until you say yes." (true: held jobs, queue)
12. **Private and shared.** "Your memory is yours. The company's is shared." (true for sources; keep it about memory, not chat)
13. **Connections.** "Your CRM, payments, forms, files. Plugged in once." (true: ready connectors). Do not show email or calendar logos.
14. **Overnight.** "At night it reads the week and writes you a letter." (true)
15. **Loop closes on Pulse.** "Tomorrow it knows more. So does your team."
16. **Close.** "One mind your company trains. Everyone's best teammate."

Do not show: voice, Slack or Teams, export, local-model chat, a score, any number.
