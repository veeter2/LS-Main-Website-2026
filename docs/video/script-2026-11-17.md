## The world (signed by Matt, 2026-11-17: "now that is badass")

A company talks, and a landscape grows out of what it said. Every sentence is a grain of sand that falls and stays. Grain with weight gathers into dunes. The model is a sun that passes over the land. The land is what you own. The world is the one the site's three paintings already show (grain, a gold road, an arch, bodies in orbit) and the one the product's own Memory draws (drawers as bodies in a constellation, an arch built one stone a month with gold brightening toward now, an aurora, a nightly letter).

One camera, no cuts. Blank paper, follow the first grain down, dunes rise, fly through the arch, up into the orbit, down into night, land on the morning road. Captions change; the world never resets. Pulse, receipts, Rules and Inbox are moments in the land, not screens.

Nothing on screen is a number, a count, a dollar figure, a real person, a real name or a real screenshot. Every screen is drawn as line-shapes, not captured. The demo account, Dana, is never named or shown. "Jev" never appears. No internal words.

## Tool: Remotion

Remotion (React) in its own folder `video/` as dev dependencies of this repo, not inside `app/`. It renders a deterministic MP4 from React code with the same Lora font and the same hex tokens, so the video and the site cannot drift. A wording change is a re-render, not a re-edit.

Output: `public/video/hero-1080.mp4` (1920x1080), `public/video/hero-720.mp4` (1280x720), `public/video/hero-poster.jpg` (first frame). H.264, 24 fps, no audio track, bitrate set to keep the 1080 file near 8 MB.

Hero wiring: `<video autoplay muted loop playsinline poster>` with a small play/pause button, loaded lazily; with reduced motion on the viewer's device only the poster shows. The four placeholder stills and `public/shots/*.jpg` are removed in the same PR.

## Cost

- Render time: roughly 3 to 6 minutes a render for the 1080 file on this Mac, about half for 720. Expect five to ten renders while tuning.
- Fonts: Lora, Google Fonts, free. No paid assets, no stock footage, no music.
- Remotion license: free for individuals and companies of up to three people. If LongStrider counts more than three people, a company license is required (priced per developer seat and per render on remotion.pro).
- Nothing else. No LLM calls, no services, no database.
