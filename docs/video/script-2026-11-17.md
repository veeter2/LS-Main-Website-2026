## Words to settle before animating

- **Pulse or Today.** The site's home page says "Today". Matt's brief says Pulse. The product tab name wins; the gap map should say which one the app shows. One word, used everywhere in the video.
- **Receipts.** The site uses "receipts"; the app chat uses "Echoes" and "Brain Receipt". The video says "receipts" because the site does.
- "Experts" and "Agents" stay as two words, as the home page lists them. In the product they share one roster, "the Team" (merged this week). If the gap map says the Team is the on-screen word, shot 12's caption becomes "The Team. Teammates with a job description."

## Tool: Remotion

Remotion (React) in its own folder `video/` as dev dependencies of this repo, not inside `app/`. It renders a deterministic MP4 from React code with the same Lora font and the same hex tokens, so the video and the site cannot drift apart. The render is a script, so a wording change is a re-render, not a re-edit.

Output: `public/video/hero-1080.mp4` (1920x1080), `public/video/hero-720.mp4` (1280x720), `public/video/hero-poster.jpg` (first frame). H.264, 24 fps, no audio track, two-pass to keep the 1080 file near 8 MB.

Hero wiring: `<video autoplay muted loop playsinline poster>` with a small play/pause button, loaded lazily, and with reduced motion on the viewer's device only the poster shows. The four placeholder stills and `public/shots/*.jpg` go in the same PR.

## Cost

- Render time: roughly 3 to 6 minutes per render on this Mac for the 1080 file, about half for 720. Expect five to ten renders while tuning.
- Fonts: Lora, Google Fonts, free. No paid assets, no stock footage, no music.
- Remotion license: free for individuals and companies of up to three people. If LongStrider, LLC counts more than three people, a company license is required (Remotion charges per developer seat and per render; price on remotion.pro). Say which applies and the cost is zero or that.
- Nothing else. No LLM calls, no services, no database.
